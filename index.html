01 — Embodied AI

Humanoid Robots

The clearest acceleration signal in this whole sweep. The segment splits into two layers: companies building the robot bodies themselves, and a "brain" layer of software that can control robots regardless of who made them — a genuine picks-and-shovels split worth keeping separate.

Humanoid robotics startups raised $8.6 billion in 2026 — 1.8x all of 2025's total, per Dealroom.

Physical Intelligence San Francisco THE "BRAIN," NOT THE HARDWARE

Doesn't build robots — builds π0, a vision-language-action model that can control any robot, regardless of manufacturer.

02 — Orbital Compute

Space Infrastructure

A genuinely new convergence trend, not just "space is hot" in general terms: analysts are specifically calling out orbital compute — data centers in orbit — as a new AI investment category.

$20.3 billion in global space funding through August 2026 — already a record year, with four months still to go.

K2 Space Los Angeles INFRASTRUCTURE PLAY

Builds large, low-cost satellite platforms ("buses") that other companies build payloads onto.

Funding$500M Series D (July 2026).
Orbital Operations USA

Developing high-assurance satellite defense to protect GPS and military communications from adversarial threats.

Leadership15+ combined years from NASA, Aerojet Rocketdyne, SpaceX, Blue Origin, and Relativity Space — exactly the kind of elite aerospace pedigree that tends to precede rapid growth.
Turion Space USA SPACEX ALUMNI

Space domain awareness, with an eye further out toward debris removal and asteroid mining.

SignalFounding team comes from SpaceX. Already $85M+ in letters of intent (LOIs).
TransAstra USA ALREADY GENERATING REVENUE

Reusable satellites for zero-g manufacturing and hypersonic technology.

Signal5 issued patents, already $5M+ in revenue — unusual for such an early-stage company in this sector.
Exosat USA

Thousands of low-cost satellites positioned as a "neutral, sovereign alternative to Starlink," entirely free of ITAR/EAR-controlled content. Plans to sell distributed compute for low-latency on-site inference to robots and autonomous vehicles — a direct bridge to the embodied AI segment above.

York Space Systems Denver ALREADY PUBLIC

Listed on the NYSE (YSS) on January 29, 2026, raising $629M. Recently acquired All.Space for $355M — a clear consolidation signal in an otherwise young sector.

03 — Deep Tech

Quantum Computing

Funding pace is actually somewhat lower in 2026 than the record year of 2025 — but what's driving the capital has matured, shifting toward infrastructure and error correction rather than hardware demonstrations alone. A more interesting signal than raw hype.

Capital is concentrating around error correction and control systems — the critical component every quantum architecture needs, regardless of which physical qubit technology ultimately wins.

Iceberg Quantum Sydney JUST OUT OF STEALTH

Building a technology-agnostic error correction layer (Pinnacle qLDPC) that works regardless of which physical qubit technology wins the race — a genuine "critical component everyone needs" position.

Funding$6M seed (2026), LocalGlobe, Blackbird, DCVC.
PartnersAlready partnered with PsiQuantum, Diraq, and IonQ.
Quantinuum USA/UK IPO WATCH

Secured a $10 billion pre-money valuation privately. Expected to go public at a level "significantly above" that.

Photonic Vancouver

A 9-year-old company with accelerating momentum rather than a brand-new name.

Funding$200M round, $2B valuation.
QuantWare Netherlands INFRASTRUCTURE PLAY

Building what it calls "the world's largest dedicated open quantum architecture fab" — another infrastructure position rather than a single hardware bet.

Already-public names to compare against: Infleqtion (NYSE: INFQ, first neutral-atom company to go public), IQM ($1.8B SPAC), Xanadu ($3.1B SPAC).
